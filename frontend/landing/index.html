AI JUDGEMENT

Claude has the final say on setup quality

The indicators shortlist three candidates. Claude looks at their recent price action and indicator scores, picks the cleanest one, or rejects all three when the market looks choppy or exhausted, and gives a short reason you can read next to the call.

Claude judges setup quality only. It cannot predict the market, and only price data is sent to it.

HOW A CALL IS MADE

The mechanism, step by step

  1. 01

    Collect

    A live feed of one minute candles for every open pair above the payout threshold. On Quotex the newest candles are built from the live tick stream so they are never stale.

  2. 02

    Measure

    RSI reads momentum, EMA 9 and 21 read trend, and MACD checks that momentum is still building. Only closed candles with no gaps are used.

  3. 03

    Vote and rank

    The three indicators vote on direction. A tie means no call. Pairs where they agree are ranked by weighted confidence and the top three go forward.

  4. 04

    Claude judgement

    Claude reviews the shortlist, picks the cleanest setup or rejects all of them, and explains its reason in a line. If the review is unavailable, the indicator ranking is used.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    The chosen pair is shown with entry at the next full minute, expiry one minute later, and a live countdown that follows the server clock.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Do the engines place trades for me?

No. They only read market data and show a call. You decide whether to trade, and you place any trade yourself on your broker.

What do BUY and SELL mean?

BUY (a "call") means the engine expects the next 1 minute candle to close higher than its entry price; SELL (a "put") means lower.

Which broker should I choose at sign in?

Sign in once and pick the broker you trade on, Pocket Option or Quotex. AUROX AI then opens with that broker's own prices, so the call lines up with the chart you trade.

Where does Claude fit in?

After the indicators shortlist the top three pairs, Claude reviews their recent price action and picks the cleanest setup, or rejects all of them. It gives a one line reason. It judges setup quality only and cannot predict the market. Only price data and indicator scores are sent to it, never your ID or any account detail.

How accurate are the signals?

We don't publish a win rate, because short term direction is hard to predict and any figure without long, independent testing would be misleading. Treat every signal as one input, size your risk accordingly, and test on a demo account.

Why did the scan say "no signal" or "warming up"?

A call needs the three indicators to agree and enough clean, recent candles. After a restart the Quotex engine needs a few minutes to rebuild live candles; at other times no pair may have a clear setup.

Can I see times in my own country?

Yes. Choose your time zone at login. Entry, expiry and history times are converted for display; the countdown always follows the server clock.
